# E-commerce Sales Analysis — Business Brief

## Context
This analysis uses a reproducible, simulated e-commerce order dataset containing 2,000 orders
from 300 customers across 2025. It is synthetic practice data, not real
customer or company data. Revenue amounts are expressed in USD.

## Key findings
- **Overall:** Total simulated revenue was $2,404,233.29 across 2,000 orders;
  average order value was $1,202.12.
- **Monthly trend:** February 2025 was the highest-revenue month
  ($232,027.20); August 2025 was the lowest
  ($179,783.75). The largest month-over-month increase was in
  February (20.0%);
  the largest decrease was in August
  (-18.9%).
- **Category performance:** Fashion generated the highest revenue
  ($853,984.93) from
  700 orders.
- **Customer concentration:** The top 30 customers (top 10% of 300 customers)
  generated 18.9% of total revenue ($455,331.97).

## Recommendations
1. **Test a loyalty initiative for high-value customers.** The top 10% generated 18.9%
   of revenue. Pilot a retention offer for this group and compare repeat orders and revenue against
   a holdout group before scaling.
2. **Prioritize the leading category.** Fashion was the largest category by revenue
   ($853,984.93). Review stock availability and
   conversion performance for this category, while monitoring margin so revenue is not mistaken for profit.
3. **Investigate monthly volatility.** Revenue ranged from $179,783.75 in
   August to $232,027.20 in February.
   Check campaign, inventory, and seasonality records around the strongest rise and fall before
   planning future promotions.

## Next steps
Validate these patterns with real, permissioned transaction data; add costs and margins; then test
the proposed actions using measurable experiments. Because this dataset is simulated, findings
describe this generated sample only and should not be treated as actual business performance.
